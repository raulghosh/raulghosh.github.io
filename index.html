Real Estate Deal Evaluator

Gives rental-property investors a buy, negotiate or pass verdict and the most they should offer. It models upkeep by the home's age, taxes for the investor's situation, the mortgage rate for their credit score, and value from zip-code sales. A built-in Claude assistant runs the model to answer what-if questions.
